How practice works

The score is a portrait page on the left. Use the score zoom controls above to enlarge it, or Fit page to see the whole sheet. The calm practice layout is always active. Full screen expands it to your display. Links with fullscreen=1 and your saved full-screen preference request full screen on your first click; browsers require an interaction. Exit full screen turns off the saved preference. Escape exits for this visit without changing it. On small screens the score and graph stack vertically.

Choose a lesson and a MIDI input, then follow the four-beat count-in. Play at the reference tempo. The middle of the cube is an exact match: green shows velocity, red shows early or late onsets, and blue shows shorter or longer key presses. Duration updates when you release a key; sustain pedal does not change it.

Matching uses pitch and the nearest unused reference onset within 500 ms. A lost place or very large timing error can therefore count as missed and extra notes. Each score starts at 100% for exact matches and falls linearly to zero at 32 velocity units, 250 ms onset error, or 100% duration error. Missing and extra notes reduce all three percentages. Unreleased notes receive no duration credit. These are practice indicators, not calibrated musical grades; MIDI velocity response varies between pianos.

Use a browser with Web MIDI support, such as desktop Chrome or Edge, and allow MIDI access. The piano must send note-on velocity and note-off messages to the computer. Listening uses a simple browser tone. No MIDI is sent to the piano.
